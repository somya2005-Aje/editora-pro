About

Editora Pro — free, ad-free, no watermark, no login, no AI. HTML/CSS/Vanilla JS. Media is processed on your device; projects save locally (IndexedDB, localStorage fallback).

⌨ Keyboard Shortcuts

Space — Play / Pause  ·  S — Split clip at playhead  ·  Delete — Delete selected clip
← / → — Step one frame  ·  Ctrl+Z — Undo  ·  Ctrl+Y — Redo
Right-click a timeline clip for Copy / Cut / Split / Separate audio / Replace and more.
