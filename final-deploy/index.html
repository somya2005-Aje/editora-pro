Frequently asked questions

Is Editora Pro really free?

Yes. Every tool is free, there are no premium locks, no subscriptions and no ads. Hosting is on Cloudflare Pages (free tier).

Will my exported videos have a watermark?

No. Exports never add a watermark — video or photo.

Do I need an account or login?

No. There is no login at all. Projects save locally on your device.

Does it use AI?

No. Editora Pro has no AI assistant, no generative AI and no AI effects, by design. Captions are manual or imported from SRT/VTT files.

Where do my files go?

Nowhere. Editing happens in your browser on your device. Files are never uploaded to a server.

Which formats can I import and export?

Import: MP4, WebM, MOV, JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF, MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG. Export: video WebM/MP4 up to 4K at 24–60 FPS; photos JPG/PNG/WebP.

Why does 4K say “depends on device”?

Browser video export renders through a canvas, and the maximum canvas size depends on your device and browser. Editora Pro renders at the highest size your device supports and tells you honestly what it used.
